Parasoul

Souls-like Boss Rush

Role

Contributed to environment and encounter design with attention to boss arena feel and player guidance.

Team

7-person UE5 team creating a combat-heavy prototype with shared design documentation.

Tools

Unreal Engine 5, environment art workflows, design documentation, and collaborative iteration.

Focus

Boss-rush pacing, combat-space readability, and mood-building through environment treatment.

Parasoul is a thrilling and high-stakes souls-like boss rush where players can battle countless challenging foes to increase in power and take on increasingly tougher enemies. Prove yourself against bosses, and equip yourself with upgrades from the market. This is a team Project, and the details are in the Game Design Document.
